How it works

Three steps to perfect AI context

1

Point to your project

Run dctx with your project path. It scans your codebase, respects ignore rules, and builds a complete snapshot.

dctx ./my-awesome-project
2

Context is ready

Structured context is copied to your clipboard instantly. Includes file tree, contents, and semantic markers for AI parsing.

Context copied! (42 files, 15.2KB)
3

Paste into any AI

Works with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, and any other AI assistant. They understand the format natively.
