dctx maintenance

View source · Edit on GitHub · Markdown · MAINTENANCE.md · 332 words · updated 7 November 2026

  • What an agreement covers
  • Tiers
  • Pricing and contact
  • Guarantees

dctx is open source under the Apache-2.0 license and free for any use, including commercial use. A maintenance agreement is how organisations that depend on dctx pay for its upkeep. Paying never changes the license and never closes a feature.

What an agreement covers#

  • Security fixes. Response targets are counted from the moment a report is confirmed:
    • Critical: a fix or mitigation within 72 hours.
    • High: a fix within 7 days.
    • Medium: a fix in the next release.
  • Long-term support. The 1.x line receives backported fixes for 18 months after 2.0 is released.
  • Priority on bug reports. Reports from covered organisations are triaged first.
  • A say in the roadmap. Covered organisations can propose priorities in a regular review. The maintainer keeps the final decision.
  • Advance notice of breaking changes. You hear about a breaking change before it ships, with a migration note.
  • Invoice and written commitment. You receive invoices and a signed maintenance commitment for procurement and vendor review.

Security reports still go through SECURITY.md, whatever your tier.

Tiers#

Tier Who it is for What you get
Supporter Individuals and teams who want to fund dctx Sponsorship and your logo in the README. No service commitments.
Team One organisation that depends on dctx Everything under "What an agreement covers", for one organisation.
Partner Organisations that build products on dctx Team, plus integration help and a named contact.

Supporter sponsorship runs through GitHub Sponsors. Team and Partner agreements are arranged directly.

Pricing and contact#

Pricing on request: info@tejl.com

Write to that address with your organisation, the tier you want and a contact name.

Guarantees#

  • Paying never changes the license. dctx stays Apache-2.0 for everyone.
  • Paying never closes or paywalls a feature. Every feature stays in the open source project.
  • The core (pack) and every modality stay open.
